## How to stock the last three weeks
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **Keep accessories deep, not full suits in long-tail sizes.** A mask, a cape and a headpiece cover more buyers per unit.
- **Reorder size-free items first.** There is no size curve to get wrong and no dead stock at the end.
- **Move the outfit-finishers to the front.** The late buyer decides at the door.
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **Bundle one mask, one cape and one headpiece** just below the sum of the parts, so a rushed buyer takes the set.
- **Stop reordering full suits mid-curve.** Late demand is thinner and harder to predict than the September run.
- **Hold glow and reflective items**, because the final week is dark by early evening.
