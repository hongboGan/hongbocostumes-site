- **Lighter colourways are recommended over dark ones**, which is a colour-range decision rather than a styling one.
- **The ask arrives from the parent, not the wearer**, and usually in the last fortnight.
@@@BLOCK@@@
## Why visibility has to be a sampling decision
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **Reflective tape is applied, not added later** \u2014 if it is missing, the buyer buys tape and sticks it on, and the costume reads homemade.
- **Glow-in-the-dark is either a print or a pigment in the yarn**, so it has to be chosen with the fabric, not after it.
- **Pale and dark colourways cannot share one spec**, and the darkest suits are the hardest case because they read dark by design.
- **The unit cost is small; the after-market fix is not**, and nothing on an unlit costume looks broken in inspection.
