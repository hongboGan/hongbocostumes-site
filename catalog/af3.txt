## The attachment specs to write into the order
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **Mechanical attachment first:** sew-on hook-and-loop, snaps with a backing plate, stitching through the seam.
- **Glue as a supplement, never a substitute** \u2014 adhered *and* stitched.
- **Abrade or prime foam and sealed surfaces** before any adhesive goes on.
- **Two attachment points instead of one**, so a single failure does not drop the piece.
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **Bar-tack every stress point** \u2014 cape shoulders, mask elastics, strap ends.
- **Detachable by design**, so a part can be removed and refitted rather than torn off.
- **A fastener kit in the carton** \u2014 a spare snap or hook-and-loop pad \u2014 so a lost part does not become a return.
