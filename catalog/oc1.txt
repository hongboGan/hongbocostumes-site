The complaint that reads as "cheap" to a buyer is rarely about price. It is about coverage: a costume that looks solid indoors and sheer in daylight, or a bodysuit thin enough that something has to be worn underneath. Coverage is settled at sampling \u2014 by fabric weight, lining and panel construction.
