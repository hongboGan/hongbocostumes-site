- **Keep pale colourways in the range**, and price them rather than dropping them.
- **Check the wearer's own vision too** \u2014 eye openings, hood depth, and a hem that clears the stairs.
- **Photograph the sample in low light**, not only on white, so the buyer sees what they are approving.

## Why it pays
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **It is the feature late buyers ask for by name**, and one of the few they will pay a little more for.
- **A treat bag or reflective accessory is a natural attach-on**, raising units per order without a new pattern.
- **It answers the plainness complaint**, which is what a fully dark costume triggers on a parent's first look.
- **It survives re-wear**, so the costume still gets used after the 31st.
