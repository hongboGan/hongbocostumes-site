## BODY

The first complaint about a costume arrives at delivery. The second arrives a month later, after the first wash or a season folded in a bag: a coated panel flaking, dark sections marking the pale ones beside them, a print that no longer matches the photo. None of it is a defect in the usual sense, and all of it is settled at sampling.

## What the care threads keep asking

- **Storage is the most common question for coated material.** People ask whether to hang the suit, box it, keep it out of sunlight, and whether a plastic bag protects it or traps moisture.
- **Dark parts marking lighter parts of the same garment** shows up repeatedly, usually after the costume has been folded away for a while.
- **Coated "leather-look" surfaces are reported to peel with age**, and the view in those threads is that once the coating starts to go, care only delays it.
- **Cleaning questions arrive attached to stains**, and the answers converge on a damp cloth, no soaking, no scratching and no direct sun.
- **Heat is the common thread in wash-damage posts** — a printed design that lasted a year can be ruined by one warm wash.

## Why care and coating are sampling decisions

- **A care label that promises more than the material can do is a return**, and the buyer who printed it pays for it.
- **Colourfastness is measurable.** Whether a black panel marks the pale one beside it is a test result, not an opinion.
- **Coating adhesion is a material choice**, so it belongs in the same conversation as price.
- **Prints are a process choice.** Heat and abrasion act differently on each print method, so the care line has to match the process used.
- **Storage guidance is part of the product** for anything coated, structured or stiffly trimmed.

## The care and durability specs to write into the order

- **Set the care instruction from the material**, not from whatever a competitor prints on a label.
- **Ask for a rub-fastness test between the darkest and lightest parts of the same style.**
- **Specify coated and knit panels by position**, so nothing is substituted at sampling.
- **Test after three washes on a production unit**, not the sealed sample.
- **Use an interleaf where a dark coated panel meets a pale one** in the bag.
- **Put the storage line on the hang tag** while the tags are being printed.

## Why it pays

- **It removes the year-two return**, which lands after the season and cannot be resold.
- **It protects the listing rating**, because the review that kills a listing is written after washing, not on arrival.
- **It is close to free at sampling** and expensive as an after-sales problem.
- **It gives your customer something to pass on**, which is what a care card is for.
