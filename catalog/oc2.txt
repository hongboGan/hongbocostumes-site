- **Buyers solve coverage themselves.** A long-running thread on revealing costumes is people adding panels, layers and longer hems after purchase.
- **The cheapest substrate is the most sheer.** Sewing threads recommend inexpensive sheer knits for costume work and note they do not fray, which is exactly why they appear in low-cost costumes.

## Why cheap costumes are see-through
@@@BLOCK@@@
- **Opacity comes from yarn mass.** A lighter knit costs less per metre and is more translucent; the two facts move together.
- **Stretch opens the fabric.** Coverage measured flat is not the coverage a wearer sees with the garment on.
- **Applied decoration needs a backing**, or the reverse of an unlined panel is left exposed.
- **Shop lighting hides it.** A costume that reads opaque at the counter can go sheer in daylight, which is where it is actually worn.
